Welcome

A church where people encounter God.

At Owode Baptist Church, we believe that the church is a place where people encounter God, grow in His Word, and find a loving community. Whether you are visiting for the first time or looking for a church family, you are welcome here.

Come and worship with us as we seek to live out the Great Commission and make a positive impact in our community.

About our church

Growing from 52 members to over 1,000.

Owode Baptist Church began on October 7, 2001, through the initiative of Saja Baptist Church, Ogbomoso. The church began with 52 members and has grown to over 1,000 members, by God’s grace.
