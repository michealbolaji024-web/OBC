Which services are in English and Yoruba?

The Sunday English Service begins at 7:30 AM and the Sunday Yoruba Service at 10:00 AM.

I am a first-time visitor. Who can help me?

Our ushers, deacons, pastor, and worship leaders will be glad to assist you. You can ask questions, request prayer, or learn more about becoming a member through our membership class.

Can I visit during the week?

Yes. We hold Bible Study and Prayer on Tuesdays at 5:00 PM, and Early Morning Prayer from Monday to Saturday, 6:00–6:45 AM.

How do I become a member?

You can learn more through our membership class. Ask any usher or church leader, or contact us. Class dates will be added once confirmed.
