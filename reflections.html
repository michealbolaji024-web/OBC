Growing in the Word

The Bible is our guide.

At Owode Baptist Church, the Bible is our guide for faith and daily living. We encourage every believer to study God’s Word, pray, and live out the teachings of Jesus Christ.

This week’s reflection.

Acts 10:38

Going about doing good

Reflect on the life and ministry of Jesus Christ, who went about doing good. May we follow His example by serving others, showing love, and sharing the hope of the Gospel.

Put the Word into practice

  • Spend time in prayer.
  • Read and reflect on Scripture.
  • Show kindness to someone.
  • Join a church gathering.
  • Find an opportunity to serve.
